# TSY Group of Companies — AI Agent Office V3.2 Rebuild Master Layout

**Status:** Approved **architectural design baseline** recorded for implementation. NOT a visually accepted release or a claim that real 3D architectural assets are already installed.

**Rollback baseline:** `release/tsy-ai-office-v3.1-frozen` at commit `710b699450398d05129fe785ae0633e0526b5df5`.

**Design source of truth:** `office-layout-v3.2.json` (this directory). This is a prototype layout specification, not building-code dimensions, occupancy approvals, or a statement of real company ownership.

## Non-negotiable architectural direction

1. **Professional architectural realism.** Use genuinely modelled 3D doors, chairs, desks, cabinetry, glazing, lighting and interiors with appropriate scale and physically plausible materials. Do **not** claim photorealism from box/cylinder placeholders. Reference photography is a design target, not proof of a coded result.
2. **Every division has a distinct plan.** A room's layout is driven by work function and hierarchy. No template copied nine times with only a name/color change.
3. **Executive differentiation.** Group Command contains a CEO office, Vice CEO office, PA station, Secretary station, executive staff area and a private boardroom.
4. **Preserve V3.1 camera contract.** Keep 3D orbit, 2D overhead pan, flat rotate, move-pad, zoom, reset, fullscreen and quick-jump controls; mobile parity must be checked.
5. **Retain existing agent roster.** Do not reset the V3 browser-local roster or change the semantics of ability ratings. Ratings, staff avatars and saved profiles do **not** assert that autonomous agents are active.
6. **Expandable architecture.** Adding a new company/division/room must create a new instance of a room template from data, not require rewriting the entire scene code. Existing IDs, assigned staff, histories and permissions must survive a layout update.
7. **No unapproved purchases or private-access tokens on GitHub Pages.** External CC0 or otherwise licensed models require provenance and load-failure handling.

Executive suite is separated from noisy project control and software areas. Finance and Governance contain private review rooms. Training and Meeting Hub can be shared across companies. Future Expansion remains unbuilt and configurable.

## Design and implementation contracts

### Room hierarchy

`Group > Company workspace > Division > Room > Staff station`.

- **Company workspace:** stable ID, display name, status, division references; company list changes must not silently alter ownership or legal entity status.
- **Division:** stable ID, unique `template`, room IDs, expandable floor assignment, permissions class, emergency fallback layout.
- **Room:** stable ID, parent division, capacity, mesh asset references, anchor points, doors/partitions, staff slot placements, camera focus, visibility.
- **Staff:** stable staff ID, company, division, room ID and workstation ID; existing ability profile (1–10) and operating instructions remain editable.
- **Asset:** URL/file identifier, license and attribution fields, geometry bounds, loading/failure behavior, LOD/mobile budget. Model must be scale-checked and inspected before merging.

### Initial floor zoning

This schedule is **conceptual**. Do not equate prototype units with building measurements.

- Quiet executive wing: Group Command + Finance & Investment + Governance.
- Active work wing: Operations + Software Development & Research + QA & Technical Test.
- Shared central hub: Meeting Hub and Connecting Centre.
- Training wing: Trainings, Upgrade & Recruitment.
- Reserved future zone: new company bays, new divisions, additional floors.

### Planned delivery gates

1. **Asset gate**: Identify and test professionally modelled furniture and architectural components. Verify actual loaded model views on desktop and iPhone; do not label external scenes realistic before rendering proof. Maintain license provenance.
2. **Group Command prototype gate**: Model six separate spaces with real doorways, believable furniture and hierarchical staff positions. Demonstrate in 3D and 2D.
3. **Navigation regression gate**: Execute orbit, pan, zoom, rotate, move, reset, fullscreen and jump tests, including touch controls. No regressions from frozen V3.1.
4. **Division individuality gate**: Build the other eight layouts according to the room schedule; confirm unique geometry, furniture placement and room functions.
5. **Agent linkage gate**: Room selection ↔ staff record ↔ editable abilities. Moving a staff member must update room assignment without deleting their profile.
6. **Expansion gate**: Add one fictitious new company workspace, one extra division and two staff records via configuration; verify old IDs and profiles are unchanged.
7. **Release gate**: Separate Patrick visual approval from operational QA. Merge only after acceptable render evidence and no functional regressions.

### Acceptance tests

- Group Command visibly contains six distinguishable subspaces, including two private offices and executive support workstations.
- Nine divisions are separately identifiable and connected by realistic circulation.
- No repeated generic three-desk module is used across every division.
- 2D overhead drag pans without tilting; 3D drag orbits; buttons remain present on iPhone.
- Save/reload/rename/edit/retire staff functions work without loss of existing local profiles.
- Agent status remains truthful; do not display 'Working' without verified GitHub/Codex execution.
- New subdivisions and subsidiaries can be introduced through the layout model with backwards-compatible IDs.
- Pages deployment and external model loading are verified. Source-code compilation alone is **not** rendering acceptance.
